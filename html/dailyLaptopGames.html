Worldle A daily geography game where you guess the country from its shape and get clues with every attempt
Flagle Guess the country by its flag colours in 6 tries.
Globle Guess the mystery country using as few guesses as possible.
Word Grid Your goal is to fill the entire grid with valid English words while aiming for the lowest possible rarity score.
Connections Connections is a word game that challenges players to find 4 themes between 16 words.
Geogrid Complete the board by filling all 9 boxes with a country that matches the criteria for the intersection of categories. Finish with the lowest (rarest) score possible.
Don't Wordle Try not to guess the hidden word. Use clues from your guesses to narrow down valid words while avoiding the Wordle answer.
Sporcle Acrostic Crack the clues, fill the grid, and watch a hidden fact emerge. First letters reveal a related clue.
